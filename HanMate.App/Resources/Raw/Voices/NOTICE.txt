HanMate bundled offline speech models

MeloTTS Chinese: MyShell.ai, MIT.
ONNX conversion: k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx.
Model: https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/vits-melo-tts-zh_en/tree/a0d5c6a264c0ef92d70d8661d8cc502d79627cd6
Output: 44100 Hz. One official Chinese female voice. No male voice is claimed.

Kokoro v1.1-zh: hexgrad, Apache-2.0.
Chinese voice data attribution: LongMaoData, as described by the model author:
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh
Pinned FP32 model (Android INT8 numerical failures were observed in device testing):
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/kokoro-multi-lang-v1_1/tree/914313412b607d95400bcd12446233fbd1248801
Unchanged voice embeddings and front-end data remain pinned to:
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/kokoro-int8-multi-lang-v1_1/tree/155831f1b4ba23b1f5c058be6a61df90cefb2a37
Output: 24000 Hz. 55 Chinese female and 45 Chinese male speaker embeddings.
Three English embeddings remain in the unchanged voices.bin but are not offered
as Chinese voices. Speaker IDs are read from the original ONNX metadata.

Each model's original LICENSE and README are included in its directory.
Models and front-end data are unmodified pinned upstream files; SHA-256 and
lengths are recorded in HanMate.Infrastructure/Voices/catalog*.json.

Runtime: sherpa-onnx 1.13.8, Apache-2.0; ONNX Runtime, Microsoft, MIT.
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.8
Kokoro requires espeak-ng front-end data (GPL-3.0-or-later); its license is included.
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
Both packs include CppJieba/jieba data; upstream MIT notices are included.
https://github.com/yanyiwu/cppjieba
https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba

This is an internal development integration. Formal content, model/data license,
redistribution obligations and listening review remain separate release gates.
No claim of final release approval or human-reviewed pronunciation is made.
All model synthesis runs locally. No user text is uploaded and no voice cloning
or pitch-shifted imitation of a missing speaker is performed.
